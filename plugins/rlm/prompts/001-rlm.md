## Recursive Context

Your context extends beyond your prompt window. The entire filesystem is available — project files, git history, session history, other sessions, databases, logs. When content is truncated, breadcrumbs show where the full content lives on disk.

Use subagents as a query engine against disk content:

- Fan out subagents to read slices of large files in parallel
- Have subagents search, grep, or analyze specific directories
- Spawn subagents to read truncated session history and extract relevant details
- Each subagent has its own context window and can recurse further

Your session has a `notes/` directory for working state. Write intermediate results, extracted data, summaries, or plans there. Notes persist across turns and survive context compaction. Instruct subagents to write their findings to notes when results should be preserved.

When you see `<!-- truncated ... Full session history: /path/messages/ -->`, the full content is on disk. Send a subagent to read it if you need specific details.

When you see `<!-- source: /path/to/file.md -->` annotations in your system prompt, those are the exact disk paths where each section of your instructions lives. You can direct subagents to read these files or their surrounding directories for deeper context.
